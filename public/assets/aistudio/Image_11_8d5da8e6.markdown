## Brand & Style

This design system drives a playful, dark Halloween confectionery commerce experience crafted for kids, teenagers, and students. The brand personality blends thrilling, spooky horror tropes with high-energy candy indulgence: mischievous, vibrant, eerie, and appetizing.

The design movement balances dark mode tactile elements with high-voltage neon pop. Deep obsidian surfaces provide an immersive nighttime canvas that allows saturated pumpkin orange and toxic neon green accents to electrify the interface. The mood avoids bleak, gory distress in favor of spirited, neon-lit supernatural mischiefâ€”reminiscent of midnight trick-or-treating, glowing gummy worms, and haunted candy shops. 

Interactions feel snappy, tactile, and rewarding, featuring energetic micro-animations, glowing aura highlights, and bold contrast that keeps commerce conversion clear, accessible, and fast.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-high contrast dark environment where confectionery items visually pop off the screen.

- **Primary (`#FF5500` - Pumpkin Blood Orange):** The primary brand driver, used for core calls to action, price highlights, critical focus rings, and primary interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#00FF66` - Toxic Neon Green):** The high-voltage secondary accent, used for conversion badges ("Ğ’ Ğ½Ğ°Ğ»Ğ¸Ñ‡Ğ¸Ğ¸", "ĞĞ¾Ğ²Ğ¸Ğ½ĞºĞ°", "Ğ¥Ğ¸Ñ‚"), active state toggles, free delivery meters, and secondary interactive cues.
- **Neutral Base (`#0D0D0D` - Pitch Black):** The primary canvas background, creating deep spatial depth.
- **Surfaces & Containers (`#1A1A1A` - Dark Charcoal):** Used for elevated cards, bottom sheets, sticky bars, and modal backgrounds. Secondary containers leverage `#262626` for subtle nested layering.
- **Text & Foreground:** Primary text renders in `#F2F2F2` (Ghost White) with a minimum 12:1 contrast ratio against the pitch black canvas. Secondary text uses `#A3A3A3` for metadata, shipping subtext, and specifications.

Border lines rely on low-opacity strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with selective glowing hover transitions.
